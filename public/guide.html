The Challenge: Why Linked-Us-In Was Built

Addressing the friction points of enterprise employee advocacy and manual content creation.

Prior to the creation of Linked-Us-In, Brother Singapore's presence on LinkedIn faced three structural hurdles identified during the Brother Xplorer POD 5 kickoff:

1. The Silent Calendar Dilemma
Posting depended on manual employee memory. Busy team leaders missed statutory milestones (e.g. Earth Day, Labour Day, SME Innovation Week), creating silence on the corporate page and employee channels.
2. The 90-Minute Blank Page Tax
Drafting an authentic, technically accurate B2B post that complies with Brother brand guidelines took technical specialists and directors between 60 to 90 minutes each week.
3. Carousel Graphic Bottlenecks
Carousels generate up to 5× more dwell time and reshares on LinkedIn, but producing 5-slide 1080×1080 graphics previously required external creative agencies or designer bandwidth.
POD 5 Core Hypothesis
By engineering an agentic content pipeline that automatically surfaces Singapore statutory holidays and live B2B industry trends, drafts Hofstede-aligned copy in 3 cultural angles, and automatically formats 5-slide editorial carousels, Brother Singapore eliminates 85% of manual drafting overhead while maintaining human review control over every post.

Template Ingestion Studio: Convert URLs, images, or PDFs into structured narrative blueprints. Click image to enlarge →

3 Ingestion Modalities

1. Live Post URL
Paste any public LinkedIn post URL. The server scrapes the raw text, extracts line-break cadence, and identifies the structural hook type.
2. Screenshot / Image Upload
Upload a PNG or JPG capture of a high-performing post. Multimodal vision parsing runs OCR and separates the copy into hook, body, and CTA.
3. Document / PDF Upload
Drop an existing multi-page slide deck. The system deconstructs the slide narrative and converts it into a 5-slide visual template.
Structural Deconstruction Output
The ingestion engine does not copy the competitor's words. It isolates the abstract structural mechanics:
• Hook Pattern: Contrarian premise + quantifiable stakes.
• Pacing Cadence: 1-sentence statements separated by double carriage returns.
• Core Value: 3 bullet points with emoji anchors (•, 🔹, 🚀).
• Call to Action: Community question directing users to comment below.
This abstract blueprint is saved directly into the Brother Singapore template library with placeholders ready for your team's content.

Support & Operations

Troubleshooting & Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Quick solutions for common operational and configuration inquiries.

Q: Magic link email is not arriving?
Solution: Check your corporate spam / junk folder for an email from Resend. Ensure your email matches an authorized domain (@brother.com.sg or @befinityai.com). In testing environments, append ?token=demo-auth&email=your.name@brother.com.sg.
Q: How do I upload carousels to LinkedIn?
Solution: LinkedIn treats carousels as document uploads. Click "Export PDF" in the Visual Studio to download the 5 slides as a single multi-page PDF, then create a post on LinkedIn and click the "Add a document" icon.
Q: Team member changes in Notion not appearing?
Solution: The browser caches team members in brother_team_cache for instant loading. Go to Team Directory and click the "Sync" button in the top right to refresh live records from Notion.
Q: Can I edit text directly in the Visual Studio?
Solution: Yes. The Visual Studio provides direct inputs for Slide Title, Eyebrow Kicker, Body Points, and CTA, allowing granular overrides for each individual slide.
