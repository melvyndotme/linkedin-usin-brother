# Brother Singapore — LinkedIn Editorial Style Guide & Persona Blueprint

## 1. Brand Identity & Persona
* **Tagline Philosophy:** *"At your side"* — empowering customers, partners, and employees with reliable, innovative, and human-centered solutions.
* **Persona Archetype:** **The Empowering Innovator & Trusted Partner**
  * Grounded, authentic, and technologically forward-thinking.
  * Respectful of heritage and precision engineering while boldly pioneering digital transformation (Brother Xplorer mindset).
  * Community-minded, embracing Singapore's multicultural harmony and forward-looking smart-nation vision.

---

## 2. Tone & Voice Attributes
| Dimension | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| **Tone** | Warm, professional, confident, approachable | Overly stiff/bureaucratic or aggressively casual/hype-driven |
| **Delivery** | Clear, concise, active voice, punchy value points | Wordy corporate jargon, passive corporate speak |
| **Perspective** | Inclusive ("We", "Our team", "Together with our partners") | Self-absorbed or transactional |
| **Innovation Stance** | Practical, empowering ("How tech unlocks human potential") | Tech-for-the-sake-of-tech or alarmist |

---

## 3. Structural Rules & Rhythms
* **The Hook (Lines 1–2):** Must stop the scroll within 120 characters before the "...see more" fold. Use a provocative question, bold insight, or warm cultural acknowledgment.
* **Body Rhythm:** Short 1–2 sentence paragraphs. Ample whitespace to maximize mobile readability.
* **Sub-Headers / Bullet Points:** Use clean formatting (•, 🔹, 🚀) to structure insights.
* **The Brother Angle:** Always connect back to human enablement, workplace productivity, or community solidarity.
* **Call to Action (CTA):** Conversational and inviting discussion (e.g., *"How is your team exploring AI in your daily workflows? Share with us below 👇"*).

## 4. Hashtag Taxonomy & Mention Conventions
* **Core Brand Tags (Always include 2–3):** `#BrotherSingapore` `#AtYourSide` `#BrotherX`
* **Topic-Specific Tags (Include 1–2):**
  * *Festive / Community:* `#SingaporeHolidays` `#SGCommunity` `#CelebrateTogether` `#LifeAtBrother`
  * *AI / Tech / Productivity:* `#FutureOfWork` `#AIProductivity` `#WorkplaceInnovation` `#DigitalTransformation`
  * *Sustainability:* `#BrotherEarth` `#SustainabilityInAction`

---

## 5. Visual Asset Guidelines
* **Primary Color Palette:** Brother Blue (`#005BAC`), Deep Navy (`#0A2540`), Slate White (`#F8FAFC`).
* **Accent Colors:** Electric Cyan (`#00D4FF`), Festive Gold (`#F59E0B`), Energetic Coral (`#EF4444`).
* **Typography:** Clean, geometric modern sans-serif (Inter / Montserrat / Arial).
* **Composition:** High contrast, clear focal points, legible on mobile screens without zooming.
