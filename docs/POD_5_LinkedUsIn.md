## 📌 The Challenge

Brother X's LinkedIn presence today relies on manual effort — someone has to remember key dates, draft a post, align it to the brand voice, get approval, and publish. This process is inconsistent, time-consuming, and often results in missed moments (a silent LinkedIn page on Earth Day, for example, is a missed brand opportunity).

**Problem statement:** There is no systematic, scalable way to generate timely, brand-aligned LinkedIn content around festive and awareness occasions — leading to inconsistent posting cadence, reliance on individual memory, and missed opportunities to build Brother's employer brand and thought leadership on LinkedIn.

**Hypothesis:** By building an agentic workflow that automatically generates LinkedIn post drafts triggered by a festive/awareness calendar, we can eliminate manual content creation effort, ensure no occasion is missed, and maintain a consistent brand presence — with humans retaining final approval before any post goes live.

## ⚠️ Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| LinkedIn API does not support draft creation | Medium | High | Validate LinkedIn API capability in Explore phase before committing to solution; fallback to email-to-reviewer workflow if API is restricted |
| AI-generated posts misaligned with Brother brand voice | Medium | High | Build brand guidelines and tone-of-voice instructions into the prompt template; human review step mandatory before any post is published |
| Festive calendar incomplete or not localised | Medium | Medium | Co-build the calendar with Chloe and HR; include both global (Earth Day) and regional (e.g. Singapore National Day) occasions |
| Low engagement from HR team post-handover | Low | High | Keep the approval workflow simple (one-click review); provide short onboarding session; designate a named reviewer in HR |
| Melvyn's availability or scope misaligned with POD timeline | Medium | Medium | Clarify engagement model, scope, and timeline with Melvyn at POD kickoff; define clear deliverables for the Explore phase |
